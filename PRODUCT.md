# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS: a single page, with no framework. Hosting is not decided yet.

Waitlist emails go to a Google Sheet or a similar simple store. The exact service and how it connects are not decided yet.

## Users

Tired nine-to-five and shift workers in Australia who struggle with groggy mornings. They mostly browse on phones, often late at night, sometimes in bed and close to sleep. The job on this page is to decide whether Yawnie is worth hearing about at launch, and if so to leave an email address.

## Product Purpose

Yawnie is a two-piece, timed-release sleep and wake gummy, currently pre-launch in the Australian market. Line: "Bear tonight. Cup at wake time."

The current surface is a pre-launch waitlist page. Success is one email address collected per interested visitor.

## Positioning

A two-piece product whose evening and morning pieces are timed to each other, presented as a bedtime-to-morning ritual, not as a supplement.

- **Visible in the renders:** the evening piece is the bear (Hugo). The morning piece is the silver capsule shaped like a lidded coffee cup.
- **Pending:** how the two pieces work and what releases when, in the user's locked wording. The user is supplying this. Do not infer it.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive on phones, frequently late at night. The page is read in the dark, by tired people, one-handed.
- The waitlist is the only action. There is no shop, pricing, menu or second call to action.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Single action:** collect one email address. Nothing else competes with it.
- **Locked copy:** all copy and ingredient wording is locked. Suggest changes to the user; never make them.
- **Mandatory disclaimers, always visible:**
  - "Concept render. The final product may look different."
  - "Times shown are examples. The release window is still being tested."
- **Regulatory/claims:** no health or benefit claims, no doses, and never the word "chamomile".
- **Undecided:** the release window, the final product appearance, the hosting, and the email service.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** Yawnie. **Character:** Hugo, a honey-gold gummy bear in a blue sleep cap, shown with his silver pill beside him.
- **Wordmark:** a large, outlined, tilted "Yawnie".
- **Label type:** DM Mono, uppercase and small, for labels and pills.
- **Brand colours (binding):** night ink `#0C1F2D`, glow `#14394B`, honey gold `#E8B84A`, morning cream `#F6E7C8`, morning sky `#BFE3EA`.
- **Personality:** playful but calm, and premium. Never clinical or "supplement-y".
- **Existing interaction:** Hugo follows the mouse. Nothing else should compete with that effect.
- **Imagery:** realistic photography of Hugo, supplied and approved. Never generate, replace or restyle images.

## Product Principles

1. **One ask.** Every element serves the single email signup. Anything that offers a second path is cut.
2. **Honest pre-launch.** The product is unfinished, and the page says so plainly. Disclaimers are part of the design, not fine print to hide.
3. **Ritual, not remedy.** Talk about tonight and the morning after, never about outcomes for the body.
4. **The user's words are final.** Copy is suggested, never rewritten.
5. **Kind to tired eyes.** It is built for a phone at midnight first, and a desktop second.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Reduced motion:** a `prefers-reduced-motion` experience is required, including a still alternative to the mouse-following Hugo.
- **Phone first:** the 390px phone layout is the primary target.
- **Night reading:** visitors read late at night on dark screens, so body text needs comfortable contrast without glare.
